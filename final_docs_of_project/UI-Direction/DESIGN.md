## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Mission
The brand embodies precision, authenticity, and refined computing performance. Grounded by the tagline *"Tech for a Better You"*, the identity merges high-end digital hardware expertise with an inviting, tactile consumer warmth. Unlike aggressive, RGB-saturated gaming retailers or cold enterprise suppliers, this system projects measured confidence, clarity, and uncompromising transparency.

### Target Audience & Tone
The target audience spans hardware enthusiasts, creative professionals, developers, and discerning PC builders seeking genuine components in the Egyptian tech marketplace. The visual tone balances technical rigor with approachability:
- **Honest & Direct:** Genuine inventory conditions, transparent pricing in EGP, and zero fabricated social proof or artificial urgency.
- **Warm Modernism:** Crisp digital layouts balanced by an organic, creamy canvas base that mitigates screen fatigue.
- **Architectural Precision:** Pixel-aligned spacing, purposeful density, and clear structural hierarchy.

### Design Movement
This design system employs **Warm Corporate Modernism**. It integrates the systematic clarity and clean component architecture of modern SaaS and e-commerce with warm natural tones, low-contrast borders, disciplined typography, and tactile surface elevations.

## Colors

### Primary Palette
- **Deep Petrol Green (`#153F3A`)**: The primary brand anchor. Used for primary navigation headers, primary CTA buttons, active state toggles, and high-emphasis typographic headers.
- **Warm Off-White (`#F6F0EB`)**: The foundational page background canvas. Softens visual contrast against pure `#FFFFFF` cards, giving the storefront its signature tactile, human feel.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Strictly designated for interactive surfaces, product cards, modal dialogs, and elevated input containers to ensure sharp product image presentation.
- **Warm Red (`#D94B3D`)**: A disciplined accent color reserved exclusively for genuine discount percentages, deal callouts, and critical validation alerts. It is never used for general decoration or false scarcity tricks.

### Structural Neutrals & Tint Layers
- **Text Primary (`#121A19`)**: High-contrast, petrol-tinted charcoal for headings and essential specs.
- **Text Secondary (`#4A5A57`)**: Balanced slate-teal for descriptive metadata, category tags, and secondary navigation links.
- **Text Muted (`#7D8C89`)**: De-emphasized metadata, unselected icons, and breadcrumb dividers.
- **Card / Surface Border (`rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.08)`)**: Low-contrast structural border providing crisp separation between white card surfaces and the warm off-white canvas.
- **Subtle Surface Tint (`rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.04)`)**: Used for table alternate rows, chip backgrounds, and disabled form elements.

## Typography

### Typographic Rationale
The storefront relies entirely on **Inter**, leveraging its tall x-height, clear numeric shaping, and open apertures. This provides optimal scanning fidelity for dense hardware model names (e.g., *RTX 4080 SUPER 16GB OC*) and currency notations.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Styled with negative letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) and tight leading to produce confident, editorial hero banners and section dividers.
- **Hardware Specs & Pricing:** Price amounts use dedicated semibold/bold tabular figures to prevent horizontal jitter during filtering and sorting. Always format pricing with currency designation: `18,450 EGP`.
- **Condition Tags & Micro Badges:** Utilize `label-sm` in uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em`) to maintain sharp legibility at small scale.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (≥1200px):** 12-column fluid grid, max container width of `1280px`, with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2.5rem` (`40px`) outer canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.5rem` (`24px`) outer margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters and `1rem` (`16px`) safe-area margins.

### Spacing Cadence
- **Component Padding:** Standard cards implement `space-lg` (`24px`) on desktop and `space-md` (`16px`) on mobile.
- **Vertical Section Rhythm:** Inter-section spacing maintains a generous `space-3xl` (`72px`) gap on desktop and `space-2xl` (`48px`) on mobile, preserving the calm, unhurried browsing experience.
- **E-commerce Shelf Arrangement:** Product grids display 4 columns on desktop, 3 columns on small desktops/large tablets, 2 columns on standard tablets, and 2 compact columns on mobile screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated via layered surfaces and ambient drop shadows rather than heavy borders or aggressive bevels.

### Layer Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (Base Level):** `#F6F0EB` (Warm Off-White) provides the base canvas tone for the storefront.
2. **Surface Level 1 (Resting Cards & Inputs):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-thin boundary: `border: 1px solid rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.08)` and subtle ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.03)`.
3. **Surface Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Flyouts):** Slight vertical lift of `-2px` with expanded shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.09), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.04)`.
4. **Surface Level 3 (Sticky Header & Floating Bars):** `#153F3A` solid or semi-opaque `#FFFFFF` with backdrop blur (`12px`), bordered by `rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.12)` with `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.08)`.
5. **Surface Level 4 (Modals & Quick View Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` anchored over a tinted backdrop overlay of `rgba(18, 26, 25, 0.6)` with `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a disciplined **Rounded** profile (`level 2`), harmonizing hardware precision with consumer friendliness:
- **Buttons & Small Interactive Controls:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius for balanced tactility.
- **Cards, Content Panels & Input Fields:** `12px` (`0.75rem`) corner radius, ensuring nested components maintain proportional inner margins.
- **Large Hero Containers & Promotion Cards:** `16px` (`1rem`) corner radius.
- **Status Tags, Condition Badges & Circular Actions:** Fully rounded pill radius (`9999px`) for badges and round floating actions (e.g., Wishlist heart icon).

## Components

### 1. Navigation Header
- **Container:** Solid Deep Petrol Green (`#153F3A`) background with crisp white typography and icons.
- **Elements:** Brand logo (cart-pixel emblem in white with `#D94B3D` accent pixel), integrated search input (light rounded container `#FFFFFF` with `#4A5A57` placeholder text), quick access links for Account, Wishlist (badge count in `#D94B3D`), and Cart drawer trigger with live item count.
- **Support Badge:** Subtly integrated WhatsApp consultation badge ("Need hardware advice? Chat on WhatsApp") styled with an inline icon and soft petrol-tinted pill surface.

### 2. Buttons & Calls to Action
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#153F3A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `8px`, height `44px` (desktop) / `48px` (mobile). Hover state darkens slightly with subtle scale transition.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#153F3A`, text `#153F3A`. Hover: background fills with `rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.06)`.
- **Accent / Sale Button:** Background `#D94B3D`, text `#FFFFFF`. Reserved solely for special flash events or direct checkout triggers.

### 3. Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.08)`, `12px` border-radius.
- **Image Area:** Neutral background pad (`#FBF9F7`), aspect ratio 1:1, smooth zoom on hover, non-distracting Wishlist heart toggle pinned to the top right.
- **Badges:** Top-left aligned.
  - *Condition:* Subdued pill badge (`New` in deep green tint, `Used - Like New` in neutral grey tint).
  - *Discount:* Warm Red (`#D94B3D`) pill containing exact discount (`-15%`). Never display artificial countdowns or faux view counters.
- **Content:** Product category in `body-sm`, bold title in `title-md` (max 2 lines clamp), specs snippet (e.g. `16GB GDDR6X | 2610 MHz`).
- **Price Block:** Large bold `price-lg` in `#121A19` (e.g., `24,500 EGP`) with strike-through previous price in `body-sm` (`#7D8C89`).
- **Action:** Full-width or corner-anchored "Add to Cart" button with clear state feedback.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Style:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.16)`, border-radius `8px`, height `44px`, font size `14px`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#153F3A` with an ambient ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.12)`.
- **Search Auto-complete:** Floating card surfacing categorized instant matches (CPUs, GPUs, Motherboards) with thumbnail, specs, and price in EGP.

### 5. Chips, Filters & Facets
- **Filter Tags:** Background `#FFFFFF` or `rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.04)`, `1px solid rgba(21, 63, 58, 0.12)`, `6px` radius. Active filter switches to background `#153F3A` with text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom styled `18px` controls with `#153F3A` checked fill and white tick icon.

### 6. WhatsApp Assistance Banner / Flow Card
- Dedicated informational card explaining order routing and direct technician assistance via WhatsApp. Styled with a crisp off-white background, subtle green borders, direct chat trigger button, and clear operating hours.
